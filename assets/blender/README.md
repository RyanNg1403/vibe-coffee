# Blender café library

The runtime library contains 79 Blender exports: eighteen patron variants, two staff
variants, two animated pets, furniture, food, plants, lighting, venue table
assemblies, a piano, a roaster, cars, and street architecture. The current pass
uses detailed character atlases, walnut, linen, stone, and Blender-baked floor
contact shading. The application assembles the room shell, signs, interactive
counter elements, laptops, and navigation around these models.

Start with the [current Blender sources and reproduction guide](realism/README.md).
The twenty people are Blender refits of two credited CC0 source characters,
with facial, hair, eyewear, headwear, and garment variants; they are not twenty
independently scanned people.
The original source GLBs remain excluded from the runtime manifest. Their
refitted `char_atelier_*` exports provide the live cast.

Editable material, character, and refined furniture files are in [realism/](realism/).
Models without a refined counterpart retain their sources in [rebuild/](rebuild/),
while the current pets live in `realism/pet_cat.blend` and
`realism/pet_dog.blend`; `cat.glb` shares the cat source.
`cafe-studio.blend` and the earlier rebuild documentation are retained as history.

## Runtime conventions

- Blender Z-up exports as glTF Y-up. Placed environment assemblies preserve
  their metre scale and authored origin; characters face glTF +Z.
- All twenty people use a compatible 24-joint source rig with `Idle`, `Walk`,
  `Working`, `Wave`, and `Sitting` actions. Staff aprons are modeled and skinned
  in Blender. Facial and clothing atlas detail is preserved.
- The cat and dog have 22-joint rigs and eight café actions. Their original
  procedural coat shaders and packed color/normal maps remain editable in
  Blender. Body bounds control runtime scale and placement.
- Walking cadence follows travel speed, rendered poses interpolate simulation
  ticks, turns ease through corners, and seating transitions blend between poses.
- A shared allocator permits at most two live instances of each character asset
  across indoor, outdoor, staff, and performer actors. Doorway transfers retain
  their existing allocation; unavailable assets reduce crowd capacity.
- Table dimensions and surface heights follow the application blueprints.
  Floor contact bakes use those layouts and the Blender furniture sources.
  Bar stools normalize to 1.10 m total height including the backrest; armchairs
  normalize to 0.89 m with the former extra 0.9 scale removed, keeping cushion
  and seated-hip heights consistent.
- Meshopt attributes are decoded before static batching or chair instancing.
  Placed-model bounds remain available for grounding audits.
- Runtime GLBs retain 1024px human atlases and pet coat color maps, 512px pet
  normal maps, and up to 512px prop maps. The packer does not upscale images.
  Identical packed images share GPU textures when their sampling settings match.

Current model sizes, triangles, and animation names are recorded in
[realism/runtime-report.json](realism/runtime-report.json), regenerated by the
asset packer. Earlier rebuild reports remain alongside their historical sources.
The standalone HTML retains all 79 models with export-only mesh and texture
compression and a 15.5 MiB transfer limit. Its losslessly compressed asset table
requires native `DecompressionStream` support in a modern browser. Full
application assets remain intact.
